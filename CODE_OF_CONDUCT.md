# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Participants must communicate constructively, respect differing viewpoints and
experiences, accept responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the
project and its users. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily
or permanently ban participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or
harmful. Report conduct concerns privately to the Vestavision organization owners.
Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the
project or community.
